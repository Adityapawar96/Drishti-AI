Face embedding model: mobilefacenet.tflite

Source package: https://github.com/Andrew-Hardianto/capacitor-face-recognition-tflite
The source package describes this MobileFaceNet asset as a 112 x 112 float32 model
with a 192-value embedding and publishes the package under the MIT License.
Its documented input normalization is (pixel / 128.0) - 1.0 and its default
cosine-similarity cutoff is 0.75. That cutoff is a starting point, not an
accuracy guarantee for this app or its camera conditions.
